Passive Voice Precision

Industrial and scientific processes require objective passive voice (Subject + is/are + Past Participle: is harvested, are roasted, is molded). The validation engine checks verb forms in real time.

Nominalization & Agent Variety

Relay Rules Summary

  • 1. Must start or include a sequencing word (First, Then, Next, After that, Finally...).
  • 2. Mostly passive voice (is/are + past participle: "is harvested", "are roasted").
  • 3. At least ONE step per team must be active/nominalized ("Workers sort..." or "After drying...").
  • 4. No repeated sequencing words within the team's paragraph.
  • 5. Each step must be at least 5 words long. First to 6 valid steps wins!

💡 Writing Hints & Formulas

1. Sequencing Words (Transitions)

First, Then, Next, After that, Subsequently, Following this, Meanwhile, In the next stage, Lastly, Finally. (Remember: Do not repeat a transition your team already used!)

2. Passive Voice Formula

Subject + is / are + Past Participle
• "The ripe berries are harvested..."
• "The dough is baked at 200°C..."
• "The liquid glass is cooled rapidly..."

3. Active or Nominalized Step (Need at least 1!)

• Active with Agent: "Workers inspect each batch by hand..." or "The machine crushes the raw beans..."
• Nominalized (-ing action noun): "After harvesting, the beans..." or "During the drying phase, moisture evaporates..."
